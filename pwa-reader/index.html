Vocabulary Level: -

?

Vocabulary Level controls which basic words are treated as already known. Changing the level changes which words may be filtered from Vocabulary Preview. It is not a test score, not a full dictionary completeness level, and can be changed anytime.

Export

Export your local word lists for review or use in other apps later. Learning TXT for 不背单词 uses one word or phrase per line. No definitions, examples, source sentences, book text, or copyrighted context are included. They are not synced or uploaded.

Backup / Restore
